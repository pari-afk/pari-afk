### hi, I'm Pari 👋

Third-year Data Science & AI student at Lakehead University. I like problems where the obvious approach is wrong, and you have to actually think about why.

**Things I've built**

🍽️ **[Toronto Restaurant Safety Scores](https://github.com/pari-afk/dinesafe_project)** — a 1-5 star safety rating for every currently operating restaurant in Toronto, built from 25 years of city inspection data. Designed the scoring methodology from scratch, caught and fixed a bug in my own formula through validation, shipped a live Streamlit dashboard covering 16,000+ restaurants. [Live demo →](https://dinesafe-project.streamlit.app)

📊 **[Olist E-Commerce SQL Analysis](https://github.com/pari-afk/olist-analysis)** — a SQL-driven analysis of a 100,000-order Brazilian marketplace. Caught a data extraction artifact that would have read as a revenue decline, and validated every finding against a second signal before trusting it.

🗞️ **The Truth Press** — a fake news detection web app using TF-IDF and a decision tree I wrote from scratch. Trained on 44,000 articles, caught a source-byline leakage issue mid-build, includes a live BBC news sidebar for real-world testing.

🧠 **Stress or Physical?** — a clinical decision support app powered by a hand-coded Bayesian network. Full auth, assessment history, and red flag detection.

🤝 **ODSC x Zerve Datathon** — team project analyzing 3.5M behavioral events to predict SaaS subscription upgrades. Engineered 30 features, removed 9 leaky events, 0.712 ROC-AUC, 4.7x lift over baseline.
